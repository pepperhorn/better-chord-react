## Bravura (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

The `music:` board icons in `src/icons.tsx` are inline SVG paths converted from
glyph outlines in [Bravura](https://github.com/steinbergmedia/bravura) by
Steinberg Media Technologies, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The source is the verovio glyph data vendored at
`packages/chordl-react/fonts/verovio/Bravura.zip`; the full licence text is at
`packages/chordl-react/fonts/OFL.txt`.

These are drawings made from the font's outlines, not a font or a derived font,
so the OFL's Reserved Font Name clause does not apply and nothing here is
distributed as Font Software. The notice is here because attribution costs
nothing and the provenance of a glyph shape is worth being able to trace.

No Bravura font file is bundled with this package.
